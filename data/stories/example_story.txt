Scene 1: The Tavern

John walked into the smoky tavern. Sarah was waiting at the corner table, her blue cloak draped over the wooden bench. The dim candlelight cast long shadows across the room as John approached.

Scene 2: The Ambush

Suddenly, bandits burst through the door. John drew his sword and pushed Sarah behind him. The tavern erupted into chaos as tables overturned and glasses shattered.

Scene 3: The Forest Escape

John and Sarah ran through the dark forest, branches whipping at their faces. The sound of pursuing bandits grew fainter as they ducked behind an ancient oak.

Scene 4: The Hidden Cave

They discovered a hidden cave behind a waterfall. Inside, ancient symbols glowed on the walls, casting an eerie blue light. John touched the symbols and felt a surge of energy.

Scene 5: The Final Stand

At dawn, they emerged from the cave to face the bandit leader. John stood tall, his scarred face set with determination. Sarah's hands glowed with magical energy as she prepared to fight.
